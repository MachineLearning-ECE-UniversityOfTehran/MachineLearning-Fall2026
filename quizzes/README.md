## Quiz–Assignment Cutoff

If a student's score on a quiz falls below the **announced quiz cutoff**, the normalized quiz score, mapped to the corresponding assignment scale, replaces the score of the corresponding assignment. The numerical cutoff and mapping details are announced through official course channels before the rule is applied.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The assessment policy assumes in-person delivery. If the course moves online or the teaching mode changes materially, quiz mechanics and related rules may be revised by the instructors. This directory is a public archive; always check eLearn and the official calendar for current instructions.
